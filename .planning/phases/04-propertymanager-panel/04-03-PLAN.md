<objective>
Deliver PROP-02 (the pink active selection-reference box) and D-10 (SolidWorks keyboard/focus semantics) on top of the left-hosted panel from Plan 04-02. `FwReferenceBoxStyler` is a thin FreeWorks styler that applies a **palette-derived pink active tone** to exactly the ONE reference box the user has clicked into — and reverts it on deactivate — while the box's actual picking machinery (fill on pick, highlight, auto-expand, clear/remove) stays OWNED by the hosted panel's `Gui::SelectionFilterGate` + `Gui::SelectionObserver` (e.g. `PartDesignGui::ReferenceSelection`).
